FarmPal Privacy Policy

How FarmPal collects, keeps, exports, and deletes farmer data — including data received through WhatsApp. Last updated: November 2026.

Data we collect

When you use FarmPal — on the web app or by messaging our WhatsApp number — we store the information you give us: your name, phone number, location, and the farm records you create (crops, expenses, harvests, sales, labour, tasks, budgets, inventory, cooperative memberships, payments, subscription and referral details).

WhatsApp messages you send to the FarmPal number (including voice notes, which are transcribed) are processed to record your farm entries and are kept in a WhatsApp event log for up to 90 days.

What you can do with your data

What deletion does

When account deletion runs, it removes your rows across all records, anonymises your user profile (“Deleted farmer”, phone and location cleared), clears your WhatsApp conversation session, and keeps only anonymised payment and audit entries required for legal and accounting purposes. After deletion nothing can identify you.

Messages and consent

Weekly/monthly summaries, renewal reminders, and crop alerts are only sent while your scheduled-messages setting is on. You can stop them any time: send STOP SUMMARIES on WhatsApp (or STOP to also block promotional messages), and START SUMMARIES to resume.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Your phone number and messages are processed through the Meta WhatsApp Business Platform solely to deliver the FarmPal service to you.

Contact

To exercise any data right — export, correction, or deletion — use the in-app options above or message DELETE MY DATA to the FarmPal WhatsApp number.
